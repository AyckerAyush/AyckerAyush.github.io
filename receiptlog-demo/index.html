Built with Zero Compromises on Privacy

Most expense apps monetize by selling your anonymized purchase habits. Receipt Scanner & Mileage Log is 100% offline, keeping your private financial records strictly on your phone.

100% Zero-Cloud Storage Guarantee

Your receipts, tax numbers, and GPS driving logs live strictly inside Android's sandboxed local SQLite database in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode. The app requires zero account creation, zero login credentials, and makes zero network calls to remote analytics or storage servers. Turn on Airplane Mode — the app works seamlessly with 100% functionality.

On-Device Neural OCR

Powered by Google ML Kit's embedded C++ neural text recognition engine. Point your camera at any receipt and extract totals, taxes, and vendor names in under 250 milliseconds without waiting for cloud processing.

Dual-Mode Mileage Tracker

Track drives either via automated foreground GPS breadcrumb logging or quick 5-second manual odometer entry. Automatically associates driving mileage with client projects and calculates tax write-offs in real time.

45+ Statutory Tax Rates
