Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, tax deductions, and offline performance.

Does this app require an internet connection or account? ▼

No. The app is engineered strictly as a 100% offline, zero-server utility. You never have to sign up, log in, or give your email. It runs completely in Airplane Mode.

How does on-device receipt OCR protect my financial privacy? ▼

Cloud scanner apps upload your private receipts to remote servers. Our app processes receipt images entirely on your phone’s local CPU/GPU using Google ML Kit. Your numbers never leave your device.

What is the difference between "Pakka (Tax Bill)" and "Kachha (Cash Memo)"? ▼

Pakka refers to formal tax invoices with official tax registration numbers (GST/HST/VAT), while Kachha refers to informal cash slips or petty cash vouchers. The app keeps them cleanly separated to prevent tax audit issues.

How do I send my tax report to my CPA or accountant? ▼

In the Reports tab, tap Export Tax PDF for a formatted audit report with receipt photos attached, or tap Export CSV for a clean spreadsheet compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, or QuickBooks.

Can I log mileage retroactively if I forgot to start GPS tracking? ▼

Yes! Use the Odometer Trip feature to enter your vehicle’s start and end odometer numbers (or exact distance traveled). The app calculates the deduction automatically.

Are there any subscription paywalls or hidden in-app purchases? ▼

No. All core features—unlimited receipt scanning, unlimited GPS mileage tracking, PDF generation, CSV exports, and full offline backups—are 100% free with zero paywalls.
