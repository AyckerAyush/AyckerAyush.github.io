Privacy Policy

Exam Photo & Sign Resizer KB (Package: com.aycker.examresizer)

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 | Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Zero Image Uploads & 100% On-Device Processing

Exam Photo & Sign Resizer KB is committed to absolute student and applicant privacy. All photo editing, background whitening, signature enhancement, DPI metadata patching, and certificate PDF compression happen 100% locally on your smartphone.

Your photographs, signatures, marksheets, and identity documents are never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or database. The core functionality operates entirely offline without requiring an active internet connection.

2. Device Storage & Saved Files

The application complies with modern Android Scoped Storage best practices and does not declare or request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE):

  • Resized Photos & Signatures: Saved directly to your device's public Pictures/ExamResizer folder using the Android MediaStore API.
  • PDF Certificates & Exam Kits (ZIP): Saved directly to your device's public Download/ExamResizer folder.
  • You maintain full control, ownership, and access to all files generated on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

Google AdMob

Used to deliver non-intrusive banner and interstitial ads for free users. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device diagnostic identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) and performance metrics. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play In-App Billing

Used to process optional Lifetime Pro upgrades. All transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment cards, banking info, or financial details.

4. Disclaimer Regarding Government Portals

Exam Photo & Sign Resizer KB is an independent productivity utility designed to help students format their application files according to published recruitment criteria. This application is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any government body, recruitment agency, or educational commission.

5. Children's Privacy

The application is designed for students and job candidates (aged 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
