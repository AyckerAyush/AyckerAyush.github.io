Privacy Policy & Legal Disclaimers

Receipt Scanner & Mileage Log (Package: com.aycker.receiptlog)

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Absolute On-Device Privacy & Zero Server Architecture

Receipt Scanner & Mileage Log is engineered strictly as a 100% offline, zero-server personal expense utility. We believe your sensitive financial receipts, tax deductions, and GPS location history should remain strictly yours.

  • No User Accounts or Passwords: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or identity details to use this application.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Scanned receipts, vendor names, line items, monetary values, and trip routes are processed and stored 100% locally on your smartphone via local SQLite database and internal app sandbox storage.
  • Zero Telemetry on Financials: The developer does NOT own, operate, or maintain any server that receives, aggregates, or monitors your financial data.

2. Device Permissions & Scoped Access

The application complies with modern Android Zero-Permission and Scoped Access requirements. It does not declare or request broad storage access permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE):

  • Camera & Photo Picker: Used exclusively when you choose to take a photo of a physical receipt or pick an existing receipt image using Android's system Photo Picker. Receipt OCR (Optical Character Recognition) runs completely on your device using on-device Google ML Kit models.
  • Foreground Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used strictly while you actively record a live business trip to calculate cumulative travel distance and compute mileage deductions. We do NOT declare, request, or use background location tracking (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION). Location sampling stops immediately when you end or pause the trip.
  • Document Picker: Used only when you explicitly export a tax PDF/CSV report or choose to restore a local encrypted backup ZIP file.
